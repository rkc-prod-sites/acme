About Acme Garments Limited

One of Fiji's most reputable garment & textile organisations.

Retail. Wholesale. Manufacturing. Three business lines, one standard of quality — delivered with a Fijian touch.

Our Story

Built on quality, service & a Fijian touch.

Acme Garments Limited is one of the most reputable and premier organisations in Fiji. Over the years the company has expanded its operations across three core areas:

1

Retail

Stores focused on both local shoppers and the tourism industry.

2

Wholesale

Supplying merchandise and hospitality textiles to retailers, hotels, resorts and island ports.

3

Manufacturing

Garment manufacturing catering for both domestic and international markets.

Our Mission

High quality products, superior customer service.

Acme Garments Limited is a strong believer in offering high quality products with superior customer service to its customers — and we do it with a Fijian touch.
